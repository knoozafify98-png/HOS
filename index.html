Club Sandwich - Prep & Dressing SOP

🥣 Ingredient Specs (Dressing):

  • • 500g Mayonnaise
  • • 30g Dijon Mustard
  • • 5ml Fresh Lemon Juice
  • • Pinch of Sea Salt & Cracked Black Pepper

🌡️ Protein Prep & Temperature Checks:

Cook Sous-vide chicken breast at 65°C for 4 hours. Rapid chill in ice bath to under 4°C. Slice precisely at 4mm thickness before line setup.

Club Sandwich - Assembly & Plating Specs

Step 1: Toasting: Toast 3 slices of toast bread to golden brown, lightly spread the dressing on each slice.
Step 2: Layering:
• Layer 1: Iceberg lettuce, 4 sliced tomatoes, 1 slice cheddar cheese, 2 slices grilled bacon.
• Layer 2: 4 chicken breast slices and freshly cooked omelette.
Step 3: Pressing & Cutting: After pressing in sandwich maker, trim crusts cleanly. Cut in half, then into equal 4 triangles. Serve with olives & iceberg lettuce with 1 cherry tomato in master pots.
